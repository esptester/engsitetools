What is earthworks visualisation?

Earthworks visualisation shows an earthworks job in 3D: the site, the machines and the work. Here's what it covers and how it compares with the alternatives.

Published 7 November 2026 · 1 min read

The short answer

Earthworks visualisation shows an earthworks job in 3D: the site, the cut and fill, and the machines doing the work. An interactive 3D simulation goes a step further. Instead of a still image or a fixed video, it's a model of the job that you can explore.

What it shows

  • The site and the ground
  • Where the cut and the fill are
  • The machines: excavators, trucks, dozers and rollers
  • Haul roads and stockpiles

Visualisation, cut and fill plan, animation or 4D BIM?

TypeWhat it is
Cut and fill planA 2D drawing coloured by cut and fill depth
AnimationA fixed video of the job, watched from start to end
4D BIMA design model linked to the construction programme, activity by activity
Interactive 3D simulationA 3D model of the job that you can explore

The difference between an animation and an interactive simulation is that an animation is a recording: you only see what the camera shows. With an interactive simulation, you can look at the part of the job you're interested in.
