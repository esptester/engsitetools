About

Built in Perth by an engineer from site.

EngSiteTools does earthworks visualisation in Perth, Western Australia, and makes engineering software for civil and construction work. Most of the apps are for the site. A few are for planning and hiring in the office.

Who's behind it

Vincent Zheng, founder.

Vincent Zheng is a mechanical engineer in Perth with more than six years in mining and civil construction. He started out on mining shutdowns, scaffolding and relining SAG mills, then worked as an underground geotechnician and as a site engineer on civil works for mining projects. Today he's a project engineer on civil earthworks projects in Western Australia.

He started EngSiteTools in 2025, and has more than two years' experience in app development and AI. Through EngSiteTools, he helps site and project teams with 3D models of earthworks jobs and machines, and with apps that give quick numbers they can check, on site and in the office. He designs and builds the apps himself.

  • Bachelor of Engineering (Mechanical), The University of Western Australia
  • Member of Engineers Australia (MIEAust)
  • Member of the AusIMM (MAusIMM)
  • Inventor on an Australian patent application for an underground mining paint applicator
  • Diploma of Leadership and Management

Why we exist

Site work gets answered on site.

Site work runs on numbers: how much to dig, how much to order, how many loads, what was tested and when. Too often those numbers live in a notebook, a spreadsheet on someone's laptop, or someone's head.

We build tools that put those answers in the hands of the people doing the work, in a form that's quick to use, easy to check, and easy to pass on.

How we build

Four rules we don't bend.

  1. 01

    Field-first

    The site apps are designed for phones, gloves and bright sun: big targets, high-contrast numbers, and nothing you don't need. If it's fiddly on site, it isn't finished.

  2. 02

    Show the working

    A number you can't check is a number you can't trust. Every result shows its inputs, its steps and its units, so it can be reviewed and signed off.

  3. 03

    Easy to pass on

    What you work out on site should flow straight into reports, orders and the site file. TrenchCalc exports its results to PDF and CSV.

  4. 04

    Private by default

    Your project data stays yours. We collect only what's needed to run the service, and we don't sell it.
