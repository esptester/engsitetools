About

Practical software for the people who build things.

EngSiteTools makes engineering software for civil and construction sites. We focus on the everyday calculations and records that keep a job moving, and we make them fast, clear and easy to check.

Why we exist

The answer belongs on site, not back at the office.

Site work runs on numbers: how much to dig, how much to order, how many loads, what was tested and when. Too often those numbers live in a notebook, a spreadsheet on someone's laptop, or someone's head.

We build tools that put those answers in the hands of the people doing the work, in a form that's quick to use, easy to check, and ready to hand over when the office needs it.

Who it's for

Made for the whole site team.

  • 01

    Site engineers

    Quantities, checks and records on the spot, without waiting to get back to a desk.

  • 02

    Supervisors & foremen

    Quick, reliable numbers for ordering, planning the day and briefing the crew.

  • 03

    Project & office teams

    Consistent records and clean exports that drop straight into reports.

How we build

Four rules we don't bend.

  1. 01

    Field-first

    Our tools are designed for phones, gloves and bright sun: big targets, high-contrast numbers, and nothing you don't need. If it's fiddly on site, it isn't finished.

  2. 02

    Show the working

    A number you can't check is a number you can't trust. Every result shows its inputs, its steps and its units, so it can be reviewed and signed off.

  3. 03

    Ready for the office

    What you work out on site should flow straight into reports, orders and the site file. Results export cleanly to PDF and CSV.

  4. 04

    Private by default

    Your project data stays yours. We collect only what's needed to run the service, and we don't sell it.
