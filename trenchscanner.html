Trench Scanner

Scan the trench. Get the volume.

Trench Scanner uses the LiDAR on your iPhone or iPad to scan an excavation and estimate how much material has come out, right there on site.

How it works

Scan, set the ground, read the volume.

For contractors, landscapers, utility crews and anyone who needs a quick volume check on site without setting up traditional survey equipment.

  1. Step 01

    Scan

    Walk around the trench from a safe distance so the camera sees the walls and the floor. LiDAR builds a 3D mesh of the site.

  2. Step 02

    Set the ground level

    Move the ground slider between the highest and lowest points of the scan to match the lip of the trench.

  3. Step 03

    Read the volume

    The app works out the excavated volume inside the scan area, in m³ or ft³.

  4. Step 04

    Check the 3D preview

    Review the mesh, the reference plane and the filled void before you leave site.

Before you start

What you need.

  • An iPhone or iPad with LiDAR, such as iPhone 12 Pro and later, or an iPad Pro with LiDAR
  • iOS 26.2 or later
  • Good light and a clear view of the trench floor

Trench Scanner gives field estimates for planning and reference. It isn't a certified survey or an engineering measurement, and results depend on scan coverage, site conditions and where you set the ground level. Follow your site safety rules, and never enter an unsafe trench to scan it.
