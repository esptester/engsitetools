FastOCR

Point the camera. Copy the text.

FastOCR gets the text out of whatever you're looking at, from documents and notes to receipts, signs, screens and handwriting, so you don't have to retype it.

What it does

No menus. Just the text.

Point the camera at the text, or pick a photo, and FastOCR recognises it in seconds. Copy it with one tap and get on with your day.

  • Real-time text recognition from the camera
  • Import a photo from your library
  • Copy the text to the clipboard in one tap
  • A clean, minimal interface with nothing in the way
  • Recognition happens on the device, and your photos aren't uploaded

Handy for documents, notes, receipts, signs, screens, books and handwriting: anything you'd otherwise retype.
