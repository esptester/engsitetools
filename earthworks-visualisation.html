  • Where the cut and the fill are

  • Haul roads and stockpiles

In the model

Move around the job, and watch it run.

  • Watch it run

    The machines load, haul, tip and return, shift after shift. Watch at real speed, or speed it up.

  • See it from anywhere

    Look over the whole site, follow a single truck, or zoom in on the loading area.

  • Change the numbers

    Change the fleet, the machines, the routes or the timings, and see how the job changes.

  • Count as it goes

    Loads, volume moved and cycle times add up as the job runs.

It opens in a web browser, with nothing to install.
