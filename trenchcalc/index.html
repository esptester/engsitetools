TrenchCalc

Scan. Calculate. Document.

Trench volumes, cement mix, culverts, spoil, and photo records, worked out on the phone while the trench is open.

01 Overview 02 Features 03 Docs 04 Help 05 Privacy 06 Terms

What it does

More than a single volume number.

Basic calculators stop at length, width, and depth. TrenchCalc keeps going into the wall shape, the fill zones, the cement, and the pipes.

  1. 01

    Volumes in seconds

    Rectangular, battered, and benched trenches. Bed, haunch, side fill, overlay, and gap zones, each with its own material.

  2. 02

    Get the mix right

    Cement percentage, water to cement ratio, and estimated 7-day and 28-day strength. A weather note for whether today is a good day to stabilise.

  3. 03

    Leave a record

    Culvert fit checks, spoil piles, a construction planner, LiDAR scans on supported phones, and photos with location, time, and direction.

How it works

Start a trench, then open the rest.

  1. Install TrenchCalc from the App Store or Google Play and open it.
  2. Tap Start Trench Setup and enter length, base width, and depth. Switch metric and imperial in settings.
  3. Read the volume, then use the home screen for walls, fill, culverts, cement, zones, and the other tools.
  4. Save the trench from the gear menu with Quick Save, or name it under Manage Saves.

Plans

Basic volumes are included. Lifetime unlocks the rest.

There is no subscription. Lifetime is a single purchase in the App Store or Google Play.

  1. Free

    Basic volumes

    Dimensions, fill type, benched and battered walls, and truck load counts.

  2. Lifetime

    The rest of the app

    Cement, culverts, spoil, soil testing, the planner, geofabric, LiDAR scanning, and photo annotation.

On the phone

Android 9 or iOS 17.

About 3 GB of RAM and 2 GB of free storage is a comfortable setup. The app itself is about 200 MB. Some paid features need a connection.

LiDAR site survey needs an iPhone 12 Pro or later, or an iPad Pro 11" or 12.9" (3rd generation or later). It is not available on every phone.
