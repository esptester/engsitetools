Questions

About the unlock.

Is it a subscription?

No. Lifetime is a single purchase through Google Play or the App Store. There is no recurring charge.

Do the basic calculations stay available?

Yes. Dimensions, fill type, and battered or benched walls stay available. Unlock the advanced features whenever you want.
