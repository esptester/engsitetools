Tools

Apps for the ground, and a few for the office.

The site apps are for the crew standing at the work. Deming+ and CFDLite are for planning and analysis at a desk.

  • On site
  • In the office

On site

For the crew at the work.

  1. 01

    TrenchCalc

    Trench and excavation volumes on site, with cement mix requirements and strength estimates. LiDAR measuring and photo records are in the paid unlock.

  2. 02

    SiteArea

    Measure an area on the map by placing points along the boundary. Free, and the layout stays on the phone.

  3. 03

    SlopeCheck

    Point the phone at the ground and get the slope angle, using LiDAR. Processed on the device.

  4. 04

    Trench Scanner

    Scan a trench with the camera and LiDAR, build a mesh, and estimate the volume dug out. A field estimate, not a certified survey.

  5. 05

    FastOCR

    Read text live from the camera. It does not upload a photo. The reading stays on the phone or tablet.

In the office

For planning and analysis at a desk.

  1. 01

    Deming+

    Prompt a plan, weigh activity risk, and generate reports. Built to sit alongside Primavera P6.

    demingplus.com →
  2. 02

    CFDLite On hold

    Fluid simulation for drag, lift and flow, without a full modelling pipeline. This one is parked.

How it works

From the tape measure to the site file.

  1. Step 01

    Enter what you see

    Type in the dimensions in front of you. Every input is labelled with its units, so there's no guessing.

  2. Step 02

    See the working

    Each result shows the steps behind it, so you can follow it line by line.

  3. Step 03

    Check it

    Inputs and outputs side by side, ready for a second set of eyes and a sign-off.

  4. Step 04

    Export and file

    Send results to PDF or CSV for reports, suppliers and the site file.
