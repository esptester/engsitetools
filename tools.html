Tools

Four toolsets for the work on the ground.

Each tool takes the dimensions in front of you and gives back an answer you can check, share and file, without a trip back to the office.

  • 01 Earthworks
  • 02 Materials
  • 03 Site records
  • 04 Survey & measurement
01

Earthworks

Excavation volumes, wall profiles, cut and fill, worked out from the dimensions in front of you.

  • Trench, pit and footing excavations
  • Battered and benched sections
  • Cut and fill quantities
  • Wall profiles from set-out dimensions
02

Materials

Quantities for fill, aggregates and stabilised mixes, and the truck loads to move them.

  • Fill and aggregates, in cubic metres or tonnes
  • Binder quantities for stabilised mixes
  • Truck loads for the trucks you've got
03

Site records

Diaries, photos and test results captured on site and kept with the job.

  • Daily diaries: weather, crew, plant and delays
  • Photos kept with the work they show
  • Test results recorded against the job
04

Survey & measurement

Measure and capture site conditions using the phone already in your pocket.

  • Quick measurements on the spot
  • Site conditions captured as you find them
  • Level checks reduced as you go

How it works

From the tape measure to the site file.

  1. Step 01

    Enter what you see

    Type in the dimensions in front of you. Every input is labelled with its units, so there's no guessing.

  2. Step 02

    See the working

    Each result shows the steps behind it, so you can follow it line by line.

  3. Step 03

    Check it

    Inputs and outputs side by side, ready for a second set of eyes and a sign-off.

  4. Step 04

    Export and file

    Send results to PDF or CSV for reports, suppliers and the site file.
