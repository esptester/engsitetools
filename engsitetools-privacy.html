EngSiteTools Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 7, 2026

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

EngSiteTools values your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and your rights as a user. We comply with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), and other applicable privacy laws. This policy applies to EngSiteTools and its products. Our product TrenchCalc™ has its own dedicated privacy policy, available at engsitetools.com/trenchcalc/privacy.html. Each of our other apps also has its own privacy policy, listed on our Privacy & legal page.

What Is Personal Data?

At EngSiteTools, we consider personal data to be any information that relates to or can identify an individual. This includes direct identifiers (like your name and email) and indirect identifiers that could reasonably be used to identify you.

Data Collection

We collect the following types of data through our website:

  • Email: When you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and the content of your message. This information is used to respond to your inquiries.
  • Server Log Data: When you visit our website, our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may automatically record standard technical information (such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested) to keep the website secure and running.
  • Cookie Data: Our website does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies, as described in the Cookies section below.

How We Use Your Personal Information

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Responding to inquiries: To answer questions you send us by email
  • Website operation and security: To keep our website secure and working properly
  • Communications: To send you information you've requested
  • Legal compliance: To meet our legal obligations

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may set strictly necessary security cookies (for example, to check that a visitor is not a bot). Because there are no optional cookies, we do not display a cookie consent banner.

Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to support our website:

Website Services

  • Google Fonts – to deliver the fonts used on our website. When a page loads, your browser requests the fonts from Google's servers, so Google receives your IP address and browser information. Requests to Google Fonts do not use cookies. See Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • Cloudflare – to host and deliver our website and protect it from attacks. Cloudflare processes standard request data (such as IP addresses) for security and reliability. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

  • Email: Emails are retained for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and are deleted within 24 months of resolving the inquiry. We do not create user accounts on our website.
  • Server Logs: Retained by Cloudflare in line with its own retention schedule

Unsolicited Personal Information

If we receive personal information that we did not actively solicit (for example, additional details included in a support email), we will assess whether we could have collected that information under APP 3. If the information is not required for our functions or activities, we will destroy or de-identify it as soon as practicable, unless we are required or authorised by law to retain it.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: All data in transit uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Access Controls: Limited access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
  • Secure Storage: All data stored with appropriate security measures

Australian Privacy Rights

If you are located in Australia or the APPs otherwise apply to you, you have the following rights:

  • Access: Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of personal information that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading.
  • Anonymity and Pseudonymity: Where lawful and practicable, you can interact with us anonymously or using a pseudonym.
  • Complaints: Lodge a privacy complaint with us and, if you are not satisfied with our response, escalate it to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable period (generally within 30 days). Further information about your rights is available from the OAIC at www.oaic.gov.au.

Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Consent: When you provide explicit consent
  • Contractual necessity: To fulfill our obligations when you use our services
  • Legal compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Legitimate interests: For website improvement and security purposes

Your Rights

If you reside in jurisdictions such as the European Union or United Kingdom, you may also have the following rights under the GDPR and comparable privacy laws (Australian-specific rights are outlined in the section above):

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal information
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal information (right to be forgotten)
  • Portability: Export your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object: Object to processing of your personal information

EngSiteTools does not sell or share personal information with advertisers.

California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Know: You can request information about the personal information we collect, use, and disclose
  • Right to Delete: You can request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: You can opt-out of the sale of your personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise your CCPA rights, see the Contact section below.

Data Processors

We use the following third-party services in connection with our website:

  • Google Fonts: Font delivery (Google receives your IP address and browser information when fonts are requested)
  • Cloudflare: Website hosting, delivery, and security (standard request data and server logs)

International Data Transfers

Your personal information may be transferred to, and processed in, countries outside your country of residence when we use the following providers:

  • United States and other countries: Google Fonts (font delivery) and Cloudflare (website hosting), which serves the website from its global network.

Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for EU/UK data transfers, our providers' data processing terms, and compliance with applicable privacy laws.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will:

  • Assess the breach and notify the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) and affected individuals in accordance with the Australian Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) scheme.
  • Where the GDPR applies, notify relevant EU/UK data protection authorities and affected users within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.
  • Notify other relevant data protection authorities where required
  • Provide details about the breach and mitigation steps
